# 🐉 Interactive Xianxia Cultivation Story Prompt

A hyper-detailed, rule-bound, choice-driven system prompt designed to turn advanced Large Language Models into an immersive, authentic, and brutally unforgiving Xianxia text-RPG. Step into a vast world of immortality where strength dictates morality and every breakthrough demands blood, time, and comprehension of the Heavenly Dao.

---

## 🤖 Recommended Models & Experience

Because of the deep mechanical tracking and state management, model selection shapes your campaign:

* **Claude (e.g., Sonnet):** **Best Overall Experience.** Delivers exceptional narrative depth and natively maintains dynamic status windows, featuring a massive context window balanced by limited chat usage.
* **Gemini (e.g., Flash Lite):** **High-Speed Iterations.** Offers unlimited chat capacity, though it operates with a smaller context window that requires careful monitoring during long campaigns.

---

## ⚙️ Setup & AI Instructions Guide

To ensure the AI permanently remembers the rules, custom parameters, and game mechanics across different chat rooms, you should place the master prompt directly into your platform's **Custom Instructions**, **System Prompts**, or **Project Knowledge** base rather than pasting it manually every time.

### How to Initialize:
1. **Save as System Prompt:** Paste the core prompt into your AI model's persistent instructions or project configuration settings.
2. **Launch a New Chat:** Open a fresh chat room where your instructions are active.
3. **Begin Your Journey:** Type the following command to auto-configure the baseline and jump straight into the action:
   > `Start or Quickstart`

The system will automatically process the opening steps, summarize your baseline parameters, and present your first scenario with the dynamic YAML status window.

## ✨ Core Features

* **Dynamic YAML Status Window:** Automatically tracks vital stats (Qi, Physical Vitality, Mental State, Divine Sense), Karmic Sin, Pill Toxicity, and active techniques.
* **Rigorous Cultivation Mechanics:** Features a Tier 1–9 organizational hierarchy, Dao Intent systems, and strict survival laws (Heart Demons, Tribulations, and Meridian Hardness).
* **True-to-Genre Paths:** Choose from Traditional Orthodox Sects, Gritty Rogue (*Sanxiu*) survival, Dynastic Clans, Demonic Paths (*Moqiu*), or Ancient Legacy Seeker journeys.
