# 🐉 Interactive Xianxia Cultivation Story Prompt

A hyper-detailed, rule-bound, choice-driven system prompt designed to turn advanced Large Language Models into an immersive, authentic, and brutally unforgiving Xianxia text-RPG. 

Step into a vast, ancient world of immortality where human life is grass, strength dictates morality, and every breakthrough demands blood, time, and comprehension of the Heavenly Dao.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Dynamic YAML Status Window:** Automatically tracks your vital stats (Qi, Physical Vitality, Mental State, Divine Sense), Karmic Sin, Pill Toxicity, active techniques, mentorship, and emerging threats in real time.
* **Rigorous Cultivation Mechanics:** Features a full Tier 1–9 organizational hierarchy (from local mortal villages to World-Dominating Immortal Palaces), Dao Intent/Comprehension systems, and Spirit Vein zoning.
* **Deep Survival Laws:** Account for **Karmic Burden & Heavenly Tribulations**, **Pill Toxicity & Meridian Hardness**, **Heart Demons (Xinmo)**, and **Time Progression & Dynamic Seclusion** (where the world shifts while you meditate).
* **True-to-Genre Tropes:** Choose your path from Traditional Orthodox Sects, Gritty Rogue (*Sanxiu*) Survival, Dynastic Aristocratic Clans, Demonic Paths (*Moqiu*), or Ancient Legacy Seeker journeys.

---

## 🤖 Recommended AI Models & Performance Notes

Because of the deep mechanical tracking and dynamic state changes, model selection matters:

* **Claude (e.g., Sonnet):** **Best Overall Experience.** Claude excels at tracking complex, multi-layered instructions and maintains the dynamic YAML status window with remarkable consistency across massive context windows. 
* **Gemini (e.g., Flash / Flash Lite):** **Great for High-Speed Iterations.** Offers rapid response times and high availability, though you should monitor context length closely during long campaigns to ensure state preservation.

## 🚀 Quickstart Guide

1. Copy the contents of `prompt.txt` (or your main prompt file).
2. Paste it into your AI chat interface (Claude or Gemini recommended).
3. **Want to jump straight into the action?** Simply type:
   > `Quickstart`
   
   The system will automatically configure the first four steps, summarize your baseline parameters, and drop you straight into your opening scenario.
4. **Want a custom journey?** Follow the Storyteller (*Oncin*) sequentially through:
   * **Step 1:** Primary Mood / Atmospheric Tone
   * **Step 2:** Story Pillars (Exploration, Combat, Social, Mysteries, Survival)
   * **Step 3:** Xianxia Path & Setting Style
   * **Step 4:** Baseline Worldview & Tier Scaling Approval
   * **Step 5–7:** Rules, Mechanics, & Character Concept Selection
   * **Step 8–9:** Status Window Verification & Opening Scene Generation

---

## 📜 Core Game Rules

* **Second-Person Immersion:** The narrative addresses you directly (*"You circulate your Qi as the elder glares..."*).
* **Strict Pacing & Consequences:** Actions carry realistic weights. Aggressive choices trigger enemy bounties, pill backlash, or sect excommunication.
* **No AI Godmoding:** The AI will *never* make choices or speak on your behalf; your progression belongs entirely to your decisions and Dao heart.
